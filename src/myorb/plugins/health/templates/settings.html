Apple Health · sleep and steps

Apple Health data stays on your iPhone, so a Shortcut sends it to myOrb every morning through a secret webhook address.

{% if webhook %}
{{ webhook }}
  1. In the Shortcuts app, create a new shortcut named Send health to myOrb.
  2. Add Find Health Samples for Steps started today, then Calculate Statistics → Sum.
  3. Add Find Health Samples for Sleep (value Asleep) started in the last 1 day, then Calculate Statistics → Sum of the duration, in minutes.
  4. Add Get Contents of URL with the address above, method POST, request body JSON with the fields date (today, as yyyy-MM-dd), steps and sleep_minutes.
  5. In Automation, run the shortcut every day at a time after you wake up, and turn off Ask Before Running.

The body can also hold resting_heart_rate, active_energy or a list of several days: {"days": [{"date": "2026-10-22", "steps": 8410, "sleep_minutes": 432}]}. Your iPhone must be able to reach myOrb, so this needs its public address.
